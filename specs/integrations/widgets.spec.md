# Home-Screen Widgets

Status: Deferred

## Product status

- A device home-screen widget is a future enhancement.
- The initial product must not depend on a widget for any required tracking
  behavior.
- All required time-tracking behavior must remain available within the
  application without installing or configuring a widget.

## Future direction

If widget work is later brought into scope, its primary purpose should be to
reduce friction for viewing paths and starting or stopping time tracking. Its
detailed behavior requires a future specification interview before
implementation.
